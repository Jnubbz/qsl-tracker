Raw Address Ripper

Logged in to QRZ as {{ qrz_username }}.

Look up a callsign against QRZ and get whatever mailing address is on file — regardless of whether they've marked it available for a direct card. The regular Dashboard hides an address unless QRZ shows an explicit accept (or at least no opt-out), which is right for "who volunteered a direct card" but useless when a contest exchange, an on-air request, or a club roster already told you a station wants a card — their QRZ page just never says "direct" anywhere. Real print-ready PDF labels, sized for one cell of an Avery 8160 / 5160 / 5260 sheet (1"×2-5/8", 30 per page, 3 columns × 10 rows). Nothing you look up or upload here is ever saved — not the address, not your ADIF file, not the callsign list. It's re-fetched from QRZ fresh every time and lives only in your own browser session.

Bulk-add from your ADIF log

Upload an ADIF (.adi/.adif) export from your own logging software and every distinct callsign in it gets a raw QRZ lookup, added straight to the batch below — handy right after a contest, when you've got a stack of cards to send out. Capped at {{ adif_upload_cap }} distinct callsigns per upload and whatever room is left on the current 30-position sheet; a callsign with no address on file, a failed lookup, or one already in the batch is skipped and called out by name rather than blocking the rest.
